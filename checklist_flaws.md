## 1. The Verdict: Honest Assessment of Your Current Checklist

**What you nailed:**
You already cover classic Vietnam traps that generic checklists miss: **rock-hard mattresses**, **solar water heaters lacking electric backups** (a disaster during Da Nang’s October–December cloudy/rainy season), **floor-shared Wi-Fi repeaters**, **20L water jug logistics**, **curfews**, and **kWh electricity markups**.

**Where it falls short:**

1. **Binary checkboxes hide dealbreakers:** Many items are simple radio/checkboxes ("AC cools fast", "Mold check"). If an apartment is 90% great but has one minor flaw, a checkbox forces the inspector to either leave it unchecked (looking incomplete) or check it anyway.
2. **Missing critical Da Nang dealbreakers:** It misses **sewer gas traps**, **wet-bath vs. separated showers**, **karaoke/empty-lot neighbors**, **window seal leaks during typhoons**, and **Foreigner Police Registration (*Khai báo tạm trú*)**.
3. **Photo vs. Video gap:** For a $1,199 sight-unseen client, static photos per section are not enough—especially for layout flow, street noise, and water pressure.

## 2. Critical Missing Checks (Must-Adds for Da Nang)

If you don't check these in Da Nang, you will end up firefighting them on WhatsApp in week two:

* **Foreigner Police Registration (*Khai báo tạm trú*):** Included right in Section 7. Does the landlord legally register foreign tenants online immediately? If a client uses your Visa-Run or visa agency partners later, missing police registration causes massive fines and visa extension blocks.
* **The "Empty Lot & Karaoke" Perimeter Check:** Checking decibels for 30 seconds at 2:00 PM won't catch the neighbor who fires up a 120dB speaker for *nhậu* (drinking/karaoke) at 8:00 PM, or an adjacent empty sand lot that is 2 weeks away from pile-driving a new 7-story hotel. Add checks for: *Adjacent empty lots / active construction within 100m* and *Local karaoke/nhậu spots on the immediate block*.
* **Bathroom Sewer Smell (P-Trap Check) & Shower Separation:** Two of the biggest Western complaints in Vietnam:
* **Floor drain traps:** Many Da Nang apartments lack S/P-traps, letting raw sewage smell rise into the bathroom every evening.
* **Shower separation:** Westerners hate when the entire bathroom floor and toilet get soaked every time they shower. Specify `[Glass Partition / Dry Toilet]`, `[Curtain Only]`, or `[Wet Room (Sprays over toilet)]`.


* **Pest Entry Points & Geckos:** Check for gaps under the main entrance door (cockroach highways), unsealed AC wall piping holes (where geckos and rats enter), and ant trails near the kitchen counter.
* **Typhoon / Rainy Season Window Seals:** In coastal areas like An Thuong and Son Tra, horizontal rain blows straight through cheap aluminum sliding windows. Inspect below window frames for **water staining, bubbling paint, or silicone peeling**.
